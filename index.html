PLANTS WITH PURPOSE

The right green
for your space.

There is no one-size-fits-all privacy hedge. Species, spacing, deer exposure, drainage, and mature dimensions all matter.

Green Giant Arborvitae

Bold, fast-growing evergreen screening where there is room to mature.

American Holly

Classic native evergreen character with year-round screening potential.

Eastern Red Cedar

A hardy native evergreen option for suitable sunny locations.

Plant recommendations are site-specific. Availability, mature growth, local conditions, and deer pressure influence final selections.
